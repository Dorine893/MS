# RRMS vs SPMS Methylation Summary

Datasets:

* GSE130029 (CD4+ T cells)
* GSE130030 (CD8+ T cells)

Major Findings:

CD4+ T Cells:

* 1 FDR-significant DMP
* Significant DMRs detected
* Evidence of regional methylation remodeling

CD8+ T Cells:

* 0 FDR-significant DMPs
* 1,512 significant DMRs
* 85 strong effect-size-filtered DMRs
* Strong immune-related signals including HLA loci

Overall Interpretation:
Regional methylation analysis is substantially more informative than single-CpG analysis in this cohort. CD8+ T cells display stronger methylation remodeling associated with disease progression from RRMS to SPMS than CD4+ T cells.
